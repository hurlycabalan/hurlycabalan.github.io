About Me

Filipino expat based in Doha, Qatar (23 years). Former B2B sales professional making a career transition into cybersecurity, targeting SOC L1 roles in Qatar and the GCC.

I deployed Microsoft's Sentinel Training Lab (six vendor data sources: CrowdStrike, Okta, Palo Alto, AWS CloudTrail, GCP, and MailGuard365) and investigated its attack scenarios. The log data is pre-recorded, so this is investigation practice rather than live attack traffic. Every investigation is documented end to end: alert triage, KQL hunting, MITRE mapping, TP/FP decision, containment, and lessons learned.

I have also simulated a brute-force attack against a test user in my own Sentinel workspace and detected it with a KQL query, with screenshots of the clean baseline and the detection hit.

No commercial SOC experience yet — but I can walk you through any scenario right now.
